1Problem identification and why it matters

Bangladesh's cottage, micro and small enterprises (shops, caterers, print studios, wholesalers, service firms) employ a large share of the country's workforce, yet almost none has a finance team. Three problems hit them at once:

  • Cash gaps on salary day. A profitable business can still miss payroll because customers pay late while suppliers and staff must be paid on time. Owners usually find out on the day, then borrow from informal lenders at high cost or delay wages.
  • Mobile-money fraud. Payments now flow through bKash, Nagad and Rocket. Shopkeepers are targeted by fake "cash-in" SMS sent from personal numbers, "I sent money to you by mistake, please send it back" calls, PIN/OTP theft by fake "office" callers, and advance-fee prize or loan scams. A single loss can wipe out a week's profit.
  • Thin credit files. Banks and microfinance institutions (MFIs) cannot see a micro-business's real cash flow, so good businesses are refused formal credit or priced as high risk.
Existing tools address one piece at a time (accounting apps, MFS awareness campaigns, credit bureaus) and mostly in English. None gives a small-business owner a forward-looking, fraud-aware view of their own cash in their own language, with guaranteed human control over money.

2Proposed AI-powered solution

MoneyGuard is a copilot that reads the business's records and messages, predicts, plans and warns, but can never move money by itself. Its workflow:

Readpaste a bKash / bank SMS or invoice; AI drafts records
CheckScam Shield screens every message for fraud
Predict14-day cash forecast, 1,000 simulated futures, late-payer model
Proposecheapest safe rescue: supplier deferral, early-pay discount, reminders
Approvea person approves with a PIN; two people for large amounts
Provesigned single-use mandate; tamper-evident audit trail
FeatureWhat the owner gets
Cash weatherThe lowest-cash day, the reserve gap and the odds of a shortfall, 14 days ahead, explained in plain words.
Rescue planAn optimiser ranks supplier deferrals, early-payment discounts and zero-cost reminders by true cost; payroll is never deferred. Simulated counterparties accept, counter or refuse.
Scam ShieldPaste any MFS SMS or caller script: verdict (likely scam / suspicious / no known red flags), risk score, reasons in English and বাংলা, and safe next steps (check the balance in the official app or USSD code; never return money because of an SMS).
Ask in বাংলা"এই মাসে কি বেতন দিতে পারব?" ("Can I pay salaries this month?") is answered in Bangla from the ledger's own numbers: payroll, risk, late payers, what-if, credit checks, rescue plans.
Credit passport and lender radarA digitally signed summary of the business's cash-flow health that a bank or MFI can verify offline; a portfolio radar shows lenders which SME clients are heading for trouble weeks early.

Expected impact (what we will measure in a pilot)

  • Earlier warning: days of notice before a reserve or payroll breach, compared with the owner's current practice.
  • Cheaper rescue: cost of the approved plan (discounts and deferrals) vs. the informal loan the owner would otherwise take.
  • Fewer fraud losses: scam messages caught before goods or money left the shop; false-alarm rate kept low so owners keep trusting it.
  • Financial inclusion: owners who use a credit passport in a bank or MFI conversation, and the outcome.
  • Financial literacy: owners who can explain their cash position after using the Bangla assistant for a month.

We do not claim user results yet: every figure in this proposal comes from synthetic test data and is labelled as such.

4Innovation and feasibility

What is new

  • One copilot for cash and fraud. Forecasting, rescue planning and MFS scam checking share the same ledger, so a suspicious "payment received" never silently fixes a forecast.
  • AI that cannot move money. The agent can only read and propose. Only a PIN-approved, signed, expiring, single-use mandate can execute; text inside a message can never grant authority.
  • Bangla-first and number-safe. Answers are templates filled with tool outputs, so translation can never change a figure.
  • Explainable fraud verdicts in two languages, with the official way to verify.
  • Private by default: ledgers stay on the owner's computer and the core works offline; cloud AI and browser voice input stay off until the owner switches them on.

Why it is feasible

  • A working prototype already exists (open source, MIT): web app, three Sylhet demo businesses in BDT, Scam Shield, Bangla assistant, treasury hub, lender radar.
  • Runs on any laptop with Python 3.11, no paid API, no internet needed for the core app.
  • 698 automated tests; a 30-case self-test blocks 25/25 simulated attacks (prompt injection, payee change, tampering, replay).
  • Security by design: role checks, CSRF, rate limits, kill switch, hash-chained audit with an independent witness.

  • Low running cost: AI runs on the user's device, so serving cost per user is near zero; no per-query cloud bill.
  • Sustainability: free core keeps micro-businesses included; lenders fund the platform because earlier warnings reduce their defaults. Open-source core builds trust; hosted sync, lender dashboards and support are paid.
  • Roadmap: (1) pilot with 20 Sylhet SMEs and one MFI; (2) consented real scam dataset and offline Bangla speech recognition; (3) read-only bank and MFS statement import with user consent; (4) regulatory review before any live payment integration. Until then, MoneyGuard stays a sandbox and moves no real money.
